# Delivery Information and Service Areas
Last Updated: 2026-08-20

## Delivery Radius and Coverage
- Our standard delivery coverage radius is up to 10 km from each restaurant kitchen branch.
- Deliveries between 10 km and 15 km are subject to long-distance availability and weather conditions.
- We do not service addresses located further than 15 km from our nearest outlet.

## Delivery Fees and Surcharges
- Standard Delivery Fee: $2.99 for orders under $25.
- Free Delivery: Orders of $25 or more qualify for free standard delivery within a 7 km radius.
- Long-Distance Surcharge: An additional $1.50 applies for delivery locations between 7 km and 15 km.
- Inclement Weather / Peak Demand Surge: A temporary fee of $1.00 - $2.00 may apply during active rainstorms or severe peak hours.

## Estimated Delivery Times and Tracking
- Typical preparation and transit time is between 30 to 45 minutes from order placement.
- Real-time GPS tracking becomes active as soon as the delivery rider picks up the order and changes the status to "out_for_delivery".
- For delayed orders exceeding 60 minutes, please submit an inquiry or contact customer support for compensation coupons.
