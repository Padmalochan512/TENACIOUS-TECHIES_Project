# Holiday Special Operating Hours and Exceptions
Last Updated: 2026-09-20

## Holiday Schedule Overrides
- On New Year's Eve, Christmas Eve, and Thanksgiving, kitchen operating hours are restricted:
  - Thanksgiving Day: 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM only.
  - Christmas Eve: 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM only.
  - New Year's Eve: 11:00 AM to 2:00 AM.
- Note: These specific holiday schedules override regular weekday/weekend operating hours listed in general store policies. When specific holiday dates apply, always follow this policy.
