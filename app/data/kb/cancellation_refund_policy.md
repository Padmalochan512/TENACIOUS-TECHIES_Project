# Cancellation and Refund Policy
Last Updated: 2026-09-15

## Order Cancellation Policy
Customers can cancel their food order directly through the app under the following rules:
- **Before Acceptance**: If the restaurant has not yet accepted your order (status is "placed"), you can cancel immediately for a 100% full refund with zero cancellation fee.
- **After Acceptance**: Once the restaurant accepts your order (status is "accepted", "preparing", or "out_for_delivery"), orders CANNOT be cancelled through the self-service app because kitchen ingredient preparation starts immediately. If you have an emergency or need special assistance after acceptance, you must contact customer support to request a human review. Cancellations approved after kitchen prep may incur up to a 50% restocking fee.
- **Delivered Orders**: Orders that are already delivered cannot be cancelled.

## Refund Timelines and Processing
- **UPI & Instant Wallets**: Refunds are processed within 2 to 4 hours of approval.
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Card refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your issuing bank.
- **Failed Transactions**: If your payment was deducted but the order status shows failed or unplaced, the payment gateway will automatically release the hold within 24 hours. If not received after 24 hours, support will initiate a manual reversal.

## Food Quality and Missing Items Policy
- If items are missing or spoiled, report the issue within 2 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.
- Full or partial refunds/credits will be issued upon support team verification within 24 hours.
