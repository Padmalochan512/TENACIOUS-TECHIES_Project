Last Updated: 2026-09-01

## Kitchen and Delivery Operating Hours
- Monday to Thursday: 10:30 AM to 11:00 PM
- Friday to Saturday: 10:30 AM to 12:30 AM (Midnight)
- Sunday: 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM
- Kitchen accepts last delivery orders 30 minutes before closing.

## Food Safety and Allergen Policy
- Common allergens handled in our shared kitchen: peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, eggs, soy, wheat, shellfish.
- If you have severe anaphylactic allergies, we advise adding explicit order notes AND calling the restaurant immediately after order placement.
- We maintain dedicated fryers and preparation tables for vegetarian items, but cross-contact cannot be 100% guaranteed in a shared commercial facility.
