# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Last Updated: 2026-09-10

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I modify my order items after checkout?
Once an order is accepted by the kitchen, modifications to items, quantities, or special instructions cannot be made directly. Please contact support immediately via ticket or chat for minor adjustments.

### How do I redeem loyalty points or promo codes?
Enter your coupon code at the checkout screen before pressing "Place Order". Coupons cannot be applied retroactively to an order that is already placed.

### What payment methods are supported?
We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and UPI. Cash on delivery is available for orders under $50 in designated zones.

### What happens if my driver cannot find my address?
The driver will attempt to call your registered phone number up to 3 times and wait for 10 minutes at the designated location.
